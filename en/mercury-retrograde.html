What is Mercury Retrograde

Three or four times a year, from Earth, the planet Mercury appears to move backward in the sky. It's an optical illusion — Mercury never actually changes direction — but its astrological effects are very real.

Mercury rules communication, contracts, travel, technology, and commerce. When it goes retrograde, all these areas call for review. Emails get lost, misunderstandings arise, phones malfunction, decisions waver.

But pay attention: Mercury Retrograde is NOT cursed. It's a cosmic period to look inward before moving forward. The keywords start with "re-": review, rewrite, reorganize, resume, reconnect.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does it mean for Mercury to be retrograde?
Mercury retrograde is an optical illusion from Earth: for a few weeks, the planet appears to move backward in the sky. Astrologically, it marks a period of review, rereading, rewriting, and reconsideration. It's not cursed — it's a period to look inward before moving forward.
How many times a year does Mercury go retrograde?
Three or four times a year, for approximately 3 weeks each time. In 2026 there are three full retrograde periods plus a few shadow days before and after.
What should I NOT do during Mercury Retrograde?
Avoid signing important contracts, buying electronics or cars, starting new projects without review, and sending impulsive emails. It's also a bad time to get married or launch a business from scratch. If you MUST sign, read it three times and keep a copy.
What should I do during Mercury Retrograde?
It's perfect for reconnecting with exes (friends, not necessarily partners), finishing pending projects, organizing papers, reading books you left unfinished, reviewing old contracts, and meditating. Words starting with "re-" work: review, rewrite, reorganize, resume.
Does it affect all signs equally?
No. It especially affects Gemini and Virgo (ruled by Mercury), and the signs where the retrograde occurs. But the real impact depends on your personal birth chart: which house Mercury retrograde is touching. A personalized transit reading tells you exactly.
How long does the effect of each retrograde last?
The direct period lasts about 3 weeks. But the "shadow period" begins about 10 days before and ends another 10 days after. In total, you will feel the energy for about 5-6 weeks.

What is the Solar Return

The Solar Return is the astrological snapshot of the exact moment the Sun returns to the position it occupied on your birth day. It occurs once a year, around your birthday, and reveals the energetic map for the next 365 days of your life.

It is not a generic horoscope. It is your personal chart for the year, calculated with Swiss Ephemeris (the same astronomical precision NASA uses) based on the place where you are on that day. That's why many people strategically travel to a specific city to activate favorable astrological houses: love, money, professional success, health.

If your birth chart is the complete script of your life, the Solar Return is the chapter of the current year.

Frequently Asked Questions about the Solar Return

What exactly is the Solar Return?
It is the astrological chart of the exact moment the Sun returns to the position it held on your birth day. It is calculated annually and reveals the energetic map of your next personal year: opportunities, challenges, encounters, and transformations from one birthday to the next.
When is the best time to do it?
Ideally, it should be done between 30 and 60 days BEFORE your birthday to know the trends of the new year. You can also do it right after if you want to confirm the direction of the months already started.
Does the place where I am on my birthday influence it?
Yes, a lot. The Solar Return is calculated based on the place where you are on your birthday. That's why many people strategically travel to a specific city to activate favorable astrological houses (this is called relocation). In your premium reading, we will tell you which city to travel to improve love, money, or health.
How is it different from my birth chart?
Your birth chart is FIXED: the map you were born with. The Solar Return is DYNAMIC: it changes every year and tells you how that birth chart will be experienced during the next 12 months, month by month.
How long does the Solar Return effect last?
Exactly 365 days. It starts on your birthday and ends the day before your next birthday. That's why it is the most accurate astrological prediction for a personal year.
Do I need to know my exact birth time?
For the complete Solar Return with astrological houses, yes. If you don't know it, we can still calculate a solar version (without houses) which is still very useful for understanding the general tone of the year.

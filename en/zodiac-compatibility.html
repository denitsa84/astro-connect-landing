Frequently Asked Questions

Which signs are most compatible?
Signs of the same element (Fire with Fire, Earth with Earth, etc.) usually have a natural affinity. The strongest classic combinations are Aries-Leo, Taurus-Capricorn, Gemini-Aquarius, Cancer-Pisces, Leo-Sagittarius, Virgo-Taurus, Libra-Gemini, Scorpio-Pisces. But real compatibility depends on the full chart, not just the Sun.
And the least compatible signs?
Traditionally, squares (signs at 90 degrees) are the most difficult: Aries-Cancer, Taurus-Leo, Gemini-Virgo, Leo-Scorpio, Virgo-Sagittarius, Libra-Capricorn. But beware: friction is not always bad, sometimes it's what makes the relationship grow.
Is Sun sign compatibility enough?
No. Real compatibility between two people is measured with synastry: the comparison of two complete birth charts. Your Sun with their Sun is only 10% of the analysis. What really matters are Venus, Mars, Moon, and the aspects between both charts.
What is synastry?
Synastry is the astrological study of the relationship between two people by comparing their complete birth charts. It analyzes how one person's planets activate the other's planets and houses. It is the most accurate way to know if two people will work out.
Can two incompatible signs work out?
Yes, perfectly. Astrology describes tendencies, not destinies. A "low Sun compatibility" can be compensated by harmony in Venus, Moon, complementary ascendants or good transits. That's why we always recommend a complete synastry before drawing conclusions.
